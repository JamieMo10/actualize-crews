          How it works

          Men never sign in or give an email. Everything runs on two things you hand them: their own link, and the QR code on the table.

          1. 1. Send the links

            Make each man his own link and send it to him directly, not in the group. On his own phone it becomes his space; anywhere else it only sends answers.

          2. 2. Show the table QR

            On the night, put the card on the table or show it on your phone. It opens three hours before the dinner and closes a day after.

          3. 3. Read the report

            See who checked in, the crew's wheel once three men have answered, and anything a man chose to share with you.

          4. 4. Between dinners

            The same link asks each man how his week went. You see the crew's average, never one man's week.

          What you can see

          • Who checked in, and who stayed to the close.
          • Whether each man did the weekly check-in, never what he said.
          • Every man's commitment and whether he kept it, so you can follow up.
          • His scores and what he brought, only where he chose to share them with you.
          • The crew's averages, once three or more men have answered.

          The men are told this on their first screen, before they answer anything.

          The connection score

          A quiet way to notice who's drifting, before it's too late to notice. Only you see it, never the men.

          Dinners, up to 40The dinners he came to, out of those held.
          Weekly check-in, up to 25The weeks he answered, out of the last four since his link was made. A week he came to dinner counts.
          Commitments, up to 15The ones he kept, out of those he's been asked about.
          Circle, up to 20When he was last on Circle, from his profile: 20 within a week, 14 within two, 7 within a month. Use Update Circle on the crew list.
          What it means70 and up is connected, 40 to 69 is worth watching, under 40 is drifting. Anything not known yet is left out, not counted against him.
          ComingLikes and comments on Circle join the score once Actualize shares Circle's own figures.

          If something goes wrong

          A man lost his linkTap Edit the list and make him a new one. His old link stops working.
          A table card went missingOpen the table QR and make a new code. Old cards and shared links stop working.
          The date or host changedOpen the dinner and use Date and host.
          Someone left the crewTap Edit the list and take him off. His past answers stay in the totals.
          Anything elseAsk Actualize.
